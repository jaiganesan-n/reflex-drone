Training · DAgger, then PPO fine-tuning

A planner teaches the pilot; practice in the game fine-tunes it

The teacher searches 1.5 s ahead and knows only what the drone's sensors and tracker know. DAgger trains arc on it; PPO then fine-tunes arc into arc_ppo.
1 · Teacher (System 2)
Beam-search planner
  • calm copies of the game, 1.5 s ahead (9 steps of 5 frames)
  • beam 2 for each first action
  • score: frames survived + clearance − move costs (bursts cost most)
  • movers planned as the tracker's predicted band
2 · Labelled data
data/teacher
  • 602,038 frames, 1,104 episodes
  • TRAIN seeds, L1–L5 mix 10/15/20/25/30%
  • random 1–5 frame action bursts show off-path states
  • per frame: soft scores, safe per action, threat, chosen action
3 · Supervised
Imitation arc_A
  • 15 epochs, AdamW, batch 512
  • KL to the soft scores + safe + threat + chosen action
  • danger and burst frames weighted 3×
  • L3 VAL: 2,009 m, 33/50 survive
4 · DAgger
Own mistakes arc_B
  • 4 rounds: arc flies, the teacher labels each state it reaches
  • retrain on all data (826k frames)
  • L5 VAL: 1,818 m, 24/50 survive
5 · Calibrate + export
artifacts/arc.onnx
  • temperature T 0.32, hesitation τ 0.45 (L5 VAL)
  • ONNX parity: logits within 1.2e-5
  • arc, the first pilot
At play time
  • Both pilots, arc and arc_ppo, fly the game: one ONNX forward pass per frame. The teacher is never called.
  • The same teacher (beam 2, replanning every 5 frames) is the yardstick for distance ratios on VAL and TEST seeds.
  • A person can only Start, Pause/Resume and End a run.
6 · PPO fine-tune
arc + PPO in the game → artifacts/arc_ppo.onnx
  • 1,000 updates, 8.19M steps: game reward + small shaping (clearance, smooth moves, fewer needless bursts)
  • + 0.5 · KL(policy ‖ arc) on its own states keeps arc's timing
  • arc_ppo, the second pilot: T 0.39, L5 VAL 1,937 m, 30/50 survive
